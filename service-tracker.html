Service Tracker

Log the job by voice. Get the invoice done.

Service Tracker is a voice job-log app for field mechanics and service techs. Talk to your phone when you arrive and when you leave, and the app keeps the time, the machine history, the photos and the notes, then turns the job into an invoice.

Features

Everything from a service call, in one place

Built for people who work with their hands, in the field, often with bad signal and no time for paperwork.

Voice check-in and check-out

Say what you're working on when you arrive and what you did when you leave. The app fills in the job details, and you can edit any field by typing.

Per-machine service history

Every visit is saved against the machine: brand, model, serial number, engine hours, problems, fault codes, work done and parts used, so the next visit starts with the full story.

Job photos and notes

Attach photos, voice memos and written notes to each job. Before-and-after photos and your findings stay with the job record.

Invoices and work reports

Turn a finished job into an invoice or work report using your labor rate, tax, trip charge and mileage settings. Print it, save it or send it from your phone.

Manager dashboard

On the Team plan, managers see jobs, check-ins, check-outs, photos and notes from their techs as they are synced, all in one place.

Works where you work

Time on the job is tracked with breaks subtracted. The app works offline and syncs when signal returns. Route and mileage tracking is optional and off by default.

Who it's for

Made for field mechanics and service techs

Independent mechanics

Mobile and field mechanics who want accurate hours and a clean invoice at the end of every job.

Service techs

Techs servicing equipment, trucks, generators, pumps, HVAC units, ag machinery and similar equipment at customer sites.

Small service teams

Owners and managers who need to know what their crew did, where, for how long, and on which machine.

Privacy

Your job records are yours

Job records are stored on your phone first. Nothing is sent to our server unless you or your team turn on sync, location tracking is off unless you turn it on, and we don't sell your information or use it for advertising. Read the full Service Tracker privacy policy.
