Service Tracker

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

A voice job log and invoicing app for field mechanics and service techs. Check in and check out by voice, and get a timed job summary and an invoice without writing it up at night.

  • Voice check-in and check-out, with every field editable
  • Time on the job, with breaks subtracted
  • Photos and voice memos attached to each job
  • Machine service history across visits
  • Optional route and mileage per job (off by default)
  • One-tap invoice or work report
  • Works offline and syncs when signal returns
