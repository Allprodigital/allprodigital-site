Service Tracker Support

Service Tracker for iPhone · Voice job log for field service work

Need help, found a problem, or have an idea? Email richard@allprodigitalco.com. We usually reply within one business day. You can also tap Send feedback in the app.

Getting started

  1. Open Service Tracker. To sync with your team, go to Settings › Sync and enter the sign-in code your manager gave you. (Working alone? You can use the app on your phone without signing in.)
  2. On Today, tap Check in. Say or type the customer or job, and the problem or notes.
  3. While you work, add parts, breaks and work notes on the job.
  4. When you're done, tap Check out and say what you did.
  5. Open the job and tap Invoice to see, print or share the invoice. Paid and unpaid invoices are under Records.

Common questions

The microphone doesn't work.

Open the iPhone Settings app › Service Tracker and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. You can always type instead.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Jobs are saved on your phone and sync when you're back online.

Where do I get a sign-in code?

Your manager creates it in the manager hub. Managers can make a new code for anyone on the team if one is lost. You can choose your own code in Settings › Change my code.

Does it track my location?

Only if you turn it on. Location at check-in, and route and mileage tracking, are both off by default and only work while the app is open. See the privacy policy.

How do I change what's on my invoices?

In Settings › You and your business: business name, phone, email, address, logo, labor rate, tax, trip charge, the invoice template, and whether times show.

How much does it cost?

Solo is $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year. Team is $39.99 a month for up to 5 members, plus $4.99 a month for each additional member, or $379.88 a year. Prices are in US dollars, and the app shows the exact price for your country before you buy. See the terms.

How do I cancel or restore a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. To cancel, open the iPhone Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Service Tracker, or tap Manage subscription in the app's Settings. Got a new phone? Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Restore purchases on the app's subscription screen. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings › Delete my account. See what gets deleted.

I'm a manager. Where's the hub?

Open the manager hub link you were given, in a browser on any computer, and sign in with your manager code. If you've lost it, email us.
