Service Tracker Privacy Policy

Service Tracker for iPhone and the Service Tracker manager hub · Last updated: November 16, 2026

Service Tracker is a job log for field service techs and small service teams. It is made by Valley Pro Logistics LLC, doing business as All Pro Digital, Rio Hondo, Texas, USA ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. Questions: richard@allprodigitalco.com.

The short version

  • Your jobs are saved on your phone first, and the app works offline.
  • If you sign in with a sign-in code, your jobs, customers and invoices sync to our Service Tracker server so you and your team's managers can see them in the manager hub.
  • Location and route tracking are off unless you turn them on.
  • Voice check-in uses Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone. The app doesn't save or send recordings of your voice.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, no analytics or advertising SDKs, and we never sell your information.
  • You can delete your account in the app: Settings › Delete my account.

What the app handles

Your account

Your name and your team (from the manager who set you up), your role (tech or manager), and your sign-in code. Our server keeps sign-in codes only as a one-way hash, not the code itself.

Jobs

Customer or job name, problem and notes, work done, parts and prices, machine details (such as brand, model, serial number and engine hours), fault codes, follow-ups, check-in and check-out times, breaks, and any edits you make.

Customers and invoices

Customer names and the contact details you enter (address, phone, email). Invoice details such as line items, labor rate, tax, trip charge, due date and paid status. The business name, phone, email, address and logo you enter in Settings are printed on your invoices. Service Tracker does not take or process payments from your customers.

Voice check-in and check-out

When you tap a microphone button, the app listens and turns your words into text using the speech recognition built into iOS. Apple may process that audio to recognize speech, under Apple's privacy policy. Service Tracker keeps only the text, never the audio, and no audio comes to us. By default the words you said stay on your phone and only the organized job details sync. You can choose to sync your raw spoken notes in Settings › Privacy. You can always type instead.

Location (optional, off by default)

  • Check-in location: if you turn on "Save my location at check-in and check-out", the app saves where you checked in and out, on your phone. It is shared with your team only if you also turn on "Share my location with my team".
  • Route and mileage: if you turn on route tracking, the app uses GPS only between check-in and check-out, and only while the app is open. Only the total miles sync, unless you turn on "Send the full route to my team".

Service Tracker asks for location only "While Using the App". It never tracks you in the background or when you're not on a job.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, we receive your message, your name and team, and a screenshot or picture only if you attach one. We use it to answer you and improve the app.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe, Apple handles the payment. We never see your card or Apple ID details. We only learn which plan is active, so we can turn on the right features.

Where it's stored

On your phone. Everything above is stored on your phone first. Settings has Export my data and Erase data on this phone.

On our server, when you sign in. Synced data is stored on our Service Tracker server, hosted by Render in the United States (Ohio), over encrypted HTTPS connections. If you never sign in, nothing is sent to us. A company can also point the app at its own server in Settings, and then its data doesn't come to us at all.

Who can see it

  • You, on your phone and with your sign-in code.
  • Managers on your team, in the manager hub: your synced jobs, customers, invoices, check-ins, check-outs and breaks, miles, and your location or full route only if you chose to share them. No team can see another team's data.
  • Us, only to run, secure and support the service.
  • Service providers that run the service for us: Render (server hosting), Apple (App Store, subscriptions and iOS speech recognition), and Google Workspace (our email, if you write to us). They may only use the data to provide their service.

We don't sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to track you. We would disclose information only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Job, customer and invoice records stay until you or your team's manager deletes them, or until the account is deleted. Full GPS routes on the server are removed after the period your company sets (90 days by default). Earlier test versions of the app could attach photos and voice memos. Version 1.0 can't attach them to jobs, and any left from test versions are removed on the same schedule or when you ask.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Turn location, route sharing and raw-note sync on or off at any time in Settings. You can also change microphone, speech recognition and location permissions in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Edit or delete your jobs in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app under Settings › Delete my account, or by email. See how account deletion works.
  • To get a copy of your data or ask a privacy question, email richard@allprodigitalco.com. We reply within 30 days.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Sign-in codes are stored only as hashes, and repeated wrong codes are blocked. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in code private.

Children

Service Tracker is a work tool for adults. It isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top. Big changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
