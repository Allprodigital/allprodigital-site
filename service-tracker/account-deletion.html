Delete your Service Tracker account

Service Tracker for iPhone · Last updated: November 16, 2026

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Service Tracker and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Delete my account.
  3. Type your sign-in code and the word DELETE, then confirm.

The phone needs a connection for this step. When it's done, your sign-in code stops working right away and the app erases your data on that phone.

What gets deleted

  • If you work alone (nobody else is on your team): your account and everything tied to it on our server are deleted. That includes your jobs, customers, invoices and invoice settings, saved jobs, routes, feedback, and your team's settings.
  • If you're on a team: your sign-in code is turned off for good, and your personal data is deleted: your name, saved locations, raw spoken notes, GPS routes, invoice settings, feedback and saved jobs. Finished jobs stay with your company for its invoices and payroll, but they no longer show your name, locations or spoken notes ("Deleted user"). Customers belong to the company and stay.
  • If you're the only manager of a team that still has other people on it, the app asks you to make someone else a manager, or remove the crew in the hub, first. That way nobody's team disappears by accident.

Nobody else's account or data is touched.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in the iPhone Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, or with Manage subscription in the app.

Can't use the app?

Email richard@allprodigitalco.com with the subject "Service Tracker account deletion", your name, and your team or business name. Please don't send your sign-in code by email. We'll confirm it's you (for example through your manager), delete the account as described above, and reply when it's done, within 30 days.

Used the app without signing in?

Then there's no account on our server. Use Settings › Erase data on this phone, or delete the app.
