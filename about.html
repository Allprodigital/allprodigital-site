About

A hands-on technology partner, wherever you are.

All Pro Digital is an owner-run technology company that designs and builds websites, apps and custom software for businesses anywhere, working remotely with clients across the U.S. and beyond.

All Pro Digital is a Valley Pro Logistics LLC company. Valley Pro Logistics LLC is the legal business behind All Pro Digital and the Service Tracker app.

Who is behind All Pro Digital

All Pro Digital is owned and run by Richard Atkinson. When you email us, you are talking to the person who will plan and build your project, not a call center or a sales team.

We work with the businesses that keep things running, from restaurants and shops to mechanics, contractors and field service companies. Many of them have no website, or track their work on paper, in text messages, or in their heads. We build practical tools that solve those problems without adding complexity.

Our first product, Service Tracker, is built on the same idea: field mechanics and service techs shouldn't have to spend their evenings writing up the day's jobs.

How we work

Plain language

We explain what we're doing and why, without jargon, and give you a written quote before work starts.

Phone first

Your customers and your crew are on their phones, so everything we build is designed for a small screen first.

Fit the way you work

We build around how your business already runs, instead of making you change everything to fit the software.

Remote and reachable

We work with clients by email and video call wherever they are, and we're easy to reach when you need a change or have a question.
