Dial Right Privacy Policy

Dial Right for iPhone · Last updated: November 3, 2026

Dial Right is a bracket-racing app that reads your drag strip time slips and suggests a dial-in. It is made by All Pro Digital, a Valley Pro Logistics LLC company based in Rio Hondo, Texas ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app handles and where it goes. Questions: richard@allprodigitalco.com.

The short version

  • Your passes stay on your iPhone. Dial Right has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. We never receive your time slips, photos or run data.
  • Location is used only to look up the weather at the track, and only while you're using the app. It is rounded to about 1 km, it isn't stored and we never see it.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don't sell or share your information.

Information the app handles

Time slips and photos

When you take or choose a photo of a time slip, Dial Right reads the numbers (reaction time, 60-foot, 330, eighth-mile and quarter-mile times and speeds, dial-in) using Apple's on-device text recognition. The photo and the numbers are saved in the app's storage on your iPhone. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Choosing a photo uses the iOS photo picker, so the app only gets the photo you pick, not your photo library.

Weather and location

To log the air conditions for each pass (temperature, humidity, barometric pressure and density altitude), Dial Right asks for your location while the app is in use. The app rounds your location to about 1 km and sends only those rounded coordinates to Open-Meteo, a free weather service, to get the current weather. When you add a pass from an older photo, the app sends Open-Meteo the photo's capture time and, if the photo has one, its location (rounded the same way) to get the weather for that time. The weather reading is saved with your pass on your phone. Your location itself is not saved, and it isn't sent to us. Open-Meteo handles the request under its own terms and privacy policy.

If you don't allow location, the rest of the app still works; weather just isn't filled in automatically.

Predictions

Dial-in suggestions are calculated on your iPhone from your saved passes and the current weather. Nothing is sent to us to make them.

Settings

Your app settings are stored on your iPhone.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in, and no Dial Right server holding your data.
  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no data brokers.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone.

Backups

Like any app's data, Dial Right's data may be included in your iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple under your Apple account settings, and we can't access them.

Deleting your information

  • Delete a single pass by swiping left on it in Past Passes, or open it and tap Delete.
  • Delete everything by deleting the Dial Right app from your iPhone. Because nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing else to delete.

Phone permissions

  • Camera: to photograph a time slip.
  • Location (while using the app): to look up track weather.

You can change these at any time in your iPhone's Settings › Dial Right.

TestFlight beta

If you test Dial Right through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares some information with us under the Apple Privacy Policy and TestFlight terms: your name and email address (if you were invited by email), the app build you installed, crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use this only to fix and improve Dial Right.

App Store crash reports

If you have turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone’s Analytics settings, Apple may share crash reports and basic usage statistics about Dial Right with us. They don’t identify you, and we use them only to fix bugs. You control this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

In-app purchases

Dial Right offers an optional one-time “Dial Right Pro” purchase. Purchases and restores are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple account; we don’t receive your name, payment details or Apple ID.

Children

Dial Right is a tool for racers and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children, and the app doesn't send personal information to us.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling information differently (for example, optional cloud backup), we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
