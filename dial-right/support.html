Dial Right Support

Dial Right for iPhone · Bracket racing ET slip scanner and dial-in helper

Need help, found a slip Dial Right misreads, or have an idea? Email richard@allprodigitalco.com. We usually reply within one business day.

If a slip reads wrong, please include a photo of the slip, the track name, and which numbers were wrong. That's the fastest way for us to fix the slip reader for your track.

Getting started

  1. Open Dial Right and tap Log New Pass on the home screen. Choose Take Photo of Slip, Choose from Photos, or Enter Manually.
  2. For photos, lay the slip flat in good light, with one slip per photo.
  3. On two-lane slips, pick your lane (Left or Right).
  4. Check every number, fix any that are wrong, and tap Save.
  5. After about five passes, the Dial In screen shows a suggested dial-in with a ± range.

Common questions

Does Dial Right need an internet connection?

Only for the automatic track weather. Slip reading, your pass log and predictions all work offline.

Why does it want my location?

Only to get the weather at the track, which affects your ET. Your location is rounded to about 1 km, isn't stored, and never comes to us. See the privacy policy.

A run was pedaled or aborted. Will it throw off my dial?

In Past Passes, swipe right on the pass and choose Exclude (or open the pass and turn on Exclude from prediction). It stays in your log but is left out of predictions. Runs far off your normal ET are also ignored automatically.

Which slips work?

Printed slips from common timing systems, including CompuLink, Accutime and RaceAmerica layouts and thermal printouts. Metric (km/h) slips are converted to mph. If a slip doesn’t read, you can type the numbers in, and please email us a photo so we can add that layout.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe left on a pass to delete it, or delete the app to remove everything. Nothing is stored on our servers.

How much does it cost?

Dial Right is free to download, and logging passes, reading slips and track weather are free. Dial Right Pro is a one-time $19.99 in-app purchase that unlocks the dial-in prediction and more than one car. There's no subscription. Already bought it? Open Settings › Dial Right Pro and tap Restore Purchases.

Can I use more than one car?

Yes. Tap the car name on the home screen and choose Add Car. Each car keeps its own passes and dial-in.

Get the app

Search for “Dial Right” on the App Store. It needs an iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
